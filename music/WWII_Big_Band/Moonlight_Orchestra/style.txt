01. Moonlight Over Manhattan
Arrangement focus: Open trumpet / sweeping reed melody
PROMPT (712 characters):
Authentic early-1940s romantic ballroom BIG BAND instrumental, smooth medium swing for couples dancing. Full orchestra: five harmonized saxophones, four trumpets, four trombones, and acoustic piano, upright bass, rhythm guitar and drums. Begin with soft piano chords and a gently rising full-reed melody; a clear open trumpet then states an original, broad skyline theme. Answer each trumpet phrase with close saxophone voicings and warm trombone swells. Add a contrasting reed-section bridge and finish with a glowing, restrained full-band chorus. Sophisticated moonlit Manhattan atmosphere. Make the layered brass-and-reed ensemble unmistakable, not a trumpet jazz quartet. No vocals or electronic instruments.
