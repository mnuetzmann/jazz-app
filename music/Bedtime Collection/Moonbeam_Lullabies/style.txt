01. Moonbeam on the Pillow
Featured sound: music box and celesta
PROMPT (547 characters):
Begin with a delicate hand-cranked music-box melody, then let a soft celesta echo its last three notes. Very slow, even rocking pulse; simple descending phrases and long, quiet spaces. A barely audible warm string pad enters halfway, then fades away. End with the original music-box motif, softly resolved. Instrumental only. Suitable for a one-year-old’s quiet bedtime: consistently low volume, no vocals, no drums, no sudden accents, no loud peaks, no abrupt tempo changes, no sound effects, no electronic beats. Warm natural acoustic recording.

Instrumental only. Featured sound: music box and muted strings
PROMPT (542 characters):

Write a sweet original melody in short phrases, with a quiet pause between each. Gradually remove the strings and finish on two soft music-box notes. Instrumental only. Suitable for a one-year-old’s quiet bedtime: consistently low volume, no vocals, no drums, no sudden accents, no loud peaks, no abrupt tempo changes, no sound effects, no electronic beats.
