01. The Stars Are Tucking In
Featured sound: warm string ensemble
PROMPT (606 characters):
A small, softly bowed string ensemble plays an original bedtime serenade in a very slow, even lullaby sway. First violins carry a simple descending melody while violas and cellos sustain gentle, consonant harmonies. No dramatic crescendos, vibrato-heavy solos or orchestral flourishes. Return to the opening theme at lower intensity and fade into a resolved ending. Instrumental only. Suitable for a one-year-old’s quiet bedtime: consistently low volume, no vocals, no drums, no sudden accents, no loud peaks, no abrupt tempo changes, no sound effects, no electronic beats. Warm natural acoustic recording.
